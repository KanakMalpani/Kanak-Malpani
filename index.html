Efficient Adaptation of Small Language Models for Reliable Mathematical Reasoning

Kanak Anil Malpani. GroundLM Workshop, ACL 2026. Published.

Six parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods, compared directly. Qwen2.5-1.5B with PiSSA reached 71.4% on GSM8K, past the unadapted 7B baseline at 68.5%. The method ranking held across three base models (Kendall’s τ = 1.0). QLoRA cut training VRAM by up to 69.6%.

What Does the Clear–Cloudy Gap Measure? A Controlled Audit of WorldFloods Flood Segmentation

CVPR 2027 submission. Under review.

The audit asks whether an apparent cloud penalty in WorldFloods v2 is actually cloud, or a difference in what the scenes contain. Scene-composition matching and same-tile synthetic cloud transplantation are the two checks.

Water IoU, clear against cloudy
Comparison Scenes Result
Raw paired scenes 8 39.3-point gap
After water-fraction matching 8 8.9-point gap
Water-fraction and NDVI co-matching 5 1.4-point gap
Same-tile transplantation, still-visible pixels 32 −5.6 points

The intervals did not support a matched cloud effect. The transplant drop reads as sensitivity to synthetic boundaries, not as proof that natural cloud causes the gap.
