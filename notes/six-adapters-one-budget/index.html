A 1.5B model passed an unadapted 7B

October 2026. GroundLM Workshop, ACL 2026. Published.

Qwen2.5-1.5B with PiSSA reaches 71.40 ± 0.27% on GSM8K. The unadapted Qwen2.5-7B sits at 68.50 ± 0.42%. Welch’s t(3.4) = 10.06, p = 0.001. Same benchmark, one training budget, three seeds. The smaller model wins after adaptation, not before it.

The comparison is the full cross-product: LoRA, QLoRA, DoRA, PiSSA, LoftQ, and AdaLoRA, on Qwen2.5-1.5B, Qwen2.5-7B, and Llama-3-8B, scored on GSM8K and MATH. Every configuration is trained on MetaMathQA. The order PiSSA, then DoRA, then a cluster of LoRA, AdaLoRA, LoftQ, and QLoRA is the same on all three base models (Kendall’s τ = 1.0). Only the PiSSA and DoRA gains over LoRA survive multiple-comparison correction. At n = 3 the other four are not separable.

Two facts sit beside the leaderboard. PiSSA’s spectral initialization at rank 16 beats LoRA at rank 64. Four-bit QLoRA cuts peak training VRAM by up to 69.6% (72.8% with PagedAdamW), within a point of the 68.7% a static-memory model predicted. PagedAdamW gives back 15–20% throughput for that headroom.
