LoopBench

A public scoreboard for feedback-driven systems. You submit a loop specification. The tasks and the seeds do not move. The rank is a number someone else can recompute.

What I built

LoopBench takes an LSS spec, runs it in LoopGym, and writes a results.json that has to match a published schema. It scores Success@k, how often the run reaches the goal, and LES_obs, an observed composite across eight categories. v0.2 accepts SimEnv submissions, which need no API keys. The package is on PyPI as loopbench. MIT.

Nineteen micro-tasks feed four suites. The generalist rank is the mean of the four suite scores, so a loop that only works in one corner does not lead the board.

The four suites

v0.2, nineteen tasks
Suite Tasks What it stresses
Repair and verify 5 Verify-driven repair, and safety
Multi-agent 5 Coordination
Research and RAG 4 Retrieval and research
Composition and safety 5 Composition, a person in the loop, memory

The board recorded on 25 June 2026 leads with a maintainer generalist LES of 86.7. That figure is the published board, not a number I am re-reporting from a fresh run here.
