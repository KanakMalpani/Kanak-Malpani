Binary-Neural-Networks

Packed binary and ternary inference for CPU and edge. The size of the weights and the speed of the kernel are different claims, and the report prints them as different numbers.

What I built

Most “binary” demos train with a straight-through estimator and then infer with sign() inside an ordinary linear layer. That is a simulation. On a commodity GPU it is often slower than FP32, and the 32× quoted in papers is usually the bit-pack, not the latency.

This lab quantizes weights to ±1, packs 64 of them into a uint64, and runs an XNOR–popcount kernel. Binary and ternary paths are both there. A report can come back REFUSE when the cosine of a drop-in wrap has collapsed, so a bad wrap is not described as a 32× win. The library is bnn, installed from PyPI as bnn-lab (the bare bnn name on PyPI is a different package). Version 1.0.0. MIT.
